# Project Statement

## Problem Statement

Keeping track of daily calorie intake is often harder than it needs to be. Most calorie tracking apps are filled with ads, require account sign-ups, or lack accurate nutritional values for common, everyday Indian home-cooked meals like dal, roti, khichdi, and poha. Because of this extra friction, people often stop logging their meals altogether.

## Scope of the Project

1. This project provides a lightweight, no-frills console program to help users log their daily food intake and calculate calories quickly.
2. It focuses on basic convenience: matching foods against a built-in list, taking custom serving quantities, and totaling the numbers instantly.
3. It does not require internet access, logins, or external database setups.
4. Future iterations can expand into saving logs across multiple days or letting users add their own custom recipes.

## Target Users

1. Students, fitness beginners, and professionals who want an easy, distraction-free way to monitor what they eat.
2. Anyone who regularly eats traditional Indian dishes and wants realistic, relatable portion estimates.
3. Minimalists who prefer a fast command-line tool over heavy, notification-heavy mobile applications.

## High-Level Features

1. Curated Indian & General Food Database: Comes pre-loaded with baseline calorie estimates for everyday meals, staples, and snacks.
2. Flexible Serving Inputs: Automatically scales the calorie count based on how many portions or servings you enter.
3. Instant Meal Tally: Shows the running addition after each meal item so you know where you stand immediately.
4. Complete Daily Recap: Summarizes all entered foods alongside individual totals and the final daily calorie sum once you finish.
